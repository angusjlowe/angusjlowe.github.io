Angus Lowe

PhD Candidate · MIT Email: alowe7 at mit dot edu


I am a PhD candidate at the Center for Theoretical Physics at MIT, advised by Aram Harrow. My primary interests lie in quantum information science, particularly quantum algorithms and quantum learning theory. Before MIT, I completed a BSc in computer science and physics at the University of Edinburgh and an MMATH at the University of Waterloo. I also previously worked as a quantum algorithms researcher at Xanadu.

I will be on the job market in the fall of 2026; feel free to get in touch!
